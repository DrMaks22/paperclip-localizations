## Remaining limits

The beta label identifies its upstream development base. It does not claim compatibility with official stable `v2026.831.1`, arbitrary newer master commits, prebuilt containers or global npm packages. Full server and deployment end-to-end suites were not newly rerun. Live provider enrollment/delivery, every possible interface state and full RTL visual support are not certified. User content, agent output and raw diagnostics retain their original language.
