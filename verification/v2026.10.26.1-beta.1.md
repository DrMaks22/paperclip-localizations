## Limits and release policy

Dependencies are prepared only in isolated checkouts using Node 24, pnpm 9.15.4, the unchanged frozen lockfile and disabled lifecycle scripts. Normal unchanged upstream build prerequisites are allowed; no dependency repair is part of this localization.

Full-monorepo/native-runner tests, Storybook visual regression, live-provider/backend E2E, production deployment and exhaustive human/visual review are not claimed. Browser fixtures contain synthetic data and have no access to a real backend, credentials or production database. User/agent content and raw provider diagnostics remain in their source language.
